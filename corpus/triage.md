## Corpus limitation to record in the Research Gap section

Only 5 of 105 papers use `agentic`; only 5 engage `offense-defense balance`
as a concept. The intersection of (a) AI autonomy, (b) offence-defence balance
theory, and (c) pre-positioning as signalling is extremely thin in this corpus.
This is itself evidence for the gap — but it also constrains what the review
can claim on the basis of these papers alone.
