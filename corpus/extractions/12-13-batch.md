## 12 — Urbanczyk, Skoumal, Elshareif, Sultana & Plass (2025)

**Citation.** Urbanczyk, J., Skoumal, C., Elshareif, M., Sultana, H., & Plass, M. R.
(2025). *State-sponsored intrusions and critical infrastructure: A case study of the
Salt Typhoon cyberattack on U.S.* TechRxiv preprint.
https://doi.org/10.36227/techrxiv.175085869.97198541/v1

**Status.** Preprint, **explicitly not peer reviewed** ("Data may be preliminary").
Undergraduate showcase paper (Lewis University "Celebration of Scholarship 2025").
**Treat as a source of documented public-record events only.**

**Framework/method.** Cyber Kill Chain + MITRE ATT&CK; open-source synthesis of CISA,
FCC, Treasury, Eclypsium, and journalism.

**Case.** Salt Typhoon: T-Mobile, Verizon, AT&T, Lumen compromised via unpatched Cisco
router vulnerabilities; GhostSpider backdoor; ~8-month undetected window (May 2023 –
Jan 2024); access to law-enforcement surveillance systems containing call metadata,
unencrypted SMS, and in some cases live call recordings.

### ★★ THE FINDING THAT MATTERS — a response asymmetry
Timeline entry, January 2024:
> "Public disclosures issued by CISA and FCC; **sanctions imposed by the U.S. Treasury
> on PRC-linked cyber entities**."

Confirmed in the results section: "The Department of the Treasury issued sanctions
against entities linked to the Chinese threat actors."

**Set this against Baram's (2026) coding of Volt Typhoon as Type 3 — technical
advisory, explicitly "no alliance-led endorsement or sanctions package followed."**

| Campaign | Character | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Volt Typhoon** (2023) | Pre-positioning for *disruption* of critical infrastructure | Joint technical advisory. **No sanctions.** |
| **Salt Typhoon** (2023–24) | *Espionage* — telecom metadata, surveillance systems | Public disclosure **+ Treasury sanctions** |

**The campaign that stole data was punished. The campaign pre-positioned for sabotage
was not.** This is the sharpest empirical evidence yet for the misrouting failure mode
identified in extraction 06, and it is a within-actor, near-contemporaneous comparison
— same adversary, same period, same target state, different response.

It also inverts the intuitive severity ordering: pre-positioning for destructive effect
against water, energy and transport is, on any strategic reading, graver than metadata
collection. The response pattern tracks **legibility and precedent**, not severity.

**Caution.** The sanctions claim is well documented in the public record (Treasury
press release JY2792; State Department release, Jan 2025) and can be cited to those
primary sources directly, bypassing this preprint entirely. **Do that.**
