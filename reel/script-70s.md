## What was trimmed, and why

Only compression — no claim was added, softened, or reversed.

- **Context** — dropped "brokered by the World Bank", "the eastern three to India, the
  western three to Pakistan" (shown on screen as a graphic instead), the exact day
  "23 April" in the read (it appears on screen and in the kicker), and India's stated
  condition for lifting the freeze.
- **Core analysis** — "large storage dams" → "storage dams"; the treaty's
  data-exchange requirement is stated as the list itself rather than described first.
- **Evidence** — "Roughly eighty percent" → "Eighty percent"; the June 2025 and
  May 2026 award dates were dropped from the read (the ruling itself is kept).
- **Why it matters / Conclusion** — "In a basin of shrinking glaciers and worsening
  floods" was cut; the closing contrast is untouched.

Everything cut from the read that carries evidentiary weight appears on screen:
the six rivers by name, the 23 April 2025 date, the 80% / quarter-of-GDP figures,
the PCA ruling and India's rejection, and the full source list.
