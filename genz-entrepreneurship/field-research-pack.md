# Field research pack

Use this to collect the primary research the deck asks for. It is built to be done in one
afternoon, mostly by phone. Everything here maps to a specific slide, so once you have the
answers you know exactly where they go.

Target sample: 12 growers, 3 commission agents (arhtis), 2 cold storage operators.
Twelve growers is enough to spot a pattern and small enough to actually finish.

---

## Part 1. Grower interview (12 people)

Aim for growers with under 5 acres who grow vegetables. Ask in Urdu or Punjabi, write the
answers in whatever language is fastest for you.

**Background**

1. How much land do you farm, and how much of it is under vegetables?
2. Which crops, and when do you harvest them?
3. Roughly how much do you pick in one harvest, in maunds or kilos?

**The 48 hour question** (this feeds slide 3)

4. After you pick, how many days can you hold the crop before it has to be sold?
5. Where does it sit in those days? Under a tree, in a room, on the trolley?
6. Has a load ever gone bad on you before you could sell it? What happened?

**Loss** (this feeds slides 2 and 9)

7. Out of a normal load, how much do you end up throwing away or selling at a throwaway price?
8. What does that cost you in rupees in a season?

**Price and timing** (this feeds slide 3)

9. Do you sell on the day you pick, or do you wait?
10. If you could hold the crop for ten more days, do you think you would get a better price?
    How much better?
11. Who decides the price you get?

**Willingness to pay** (this is the single most important answer in the whole pack, slide 5)

12. If there was a cold room in this village and you could keep ten crates there for
    PKR 12 per crate per day, would you use it?
13. If not at PKR 12, at what price would you use it?
14. Would you pay in advance, or after you sell?
15. What would stop you from trusting it with your crop?

**Aggregation** (this feeds slide 7 and the revenue model)

16. If we graded and sold your crop in the city for you and kept 7 percent, would you use that,
    or would you rather sell it yourself?

## Part 2. Commission agent interview (3 people)

Do not go in as if you are there to replace him. You are not, and he will help you more if
he understands that.

1. How many growers do you deal with in a normal season?
2. Do you advance money or inputs before the crop is sown? On what terms?
3. What happens to produce that arrives in poor condition?
4. Do you use cold storage yourself? Where, and what does it cost you?
5. If there was a small cold room in the village, would you rent space in it?
6. What would you want from us before you sent your growers to it?

---

## Part 3. Cold storage operator interview (2 people)

Any commercial cold storage near you will do, even a large one.

1. What is your capacity and what do you charge, per kg or per bag, per month?
2. What is the smallest quantity you will accept?
3. Do you take produce for a few days, or only for a season?
4. What does electricity cost you in a month, and how much does load shedding hurt you?
5. What did the facility cost to build, and what did the machinery cost?
6. What breaks most often?

## Part 4. What to do with the answers

| Question | Goes into |
|---|---|
| Q4, Q5 | Slide 3, the 48 hour window. Replace our description with what growers actually said. |
| Q7, Q8 | Slide 2 and slide 9. Replace the 25 percent spoilage assumption with your own number. |
| Q12, Q13 | Slide 5, first and second finding box. This is the number that decides the pitch. |
| Q10 | Slide 9, the upside line. |
| Part 2 Q5 | Slide 11, the commission agent card. |
| Part 3 Q1, Q2, Q5 | Slide 4 and slide 10. Replace the PKR 2.5 million estimate if you get a real build cost. |

Write the three finding boxes on slide 5 as short sentences with a number in them, for example
"9 of 12 said they must sell within two days" rather than "most growers sell quickly." Numbers
are what the panel will remember.
